The Science

Why 4 Seasons
Change Everything

A single satellite photo is not enough. Olive trees look identical to almonds in summer. But across a full year, their growth patterns are completely unique.

🌸
Phenological Analysis
By fusing 4 seasonal images into a 56-band tensor, the model learns the full lifecycle of each crop — not just one frozen moment in time.
🛡️
False Positive Rejection
Almond orchards look identical to olive groves in a single photo. The model rejects them because their seasonal lifecycle is completely different.
📡
Sentinel-2 Multispectral
10m resolution, 10 bands per season including NIR, SWIR, and Red Edge — plus 4 computed vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, NDWI, SAVI).
