Real-Time Pipeline

From Your Drawing
to Detection in Seconds

No files to download. No pre-loaded data. Just draw, and let the cloud do the rest.

01 ✏️

Draw Anywhere

Draw a small rectangle anywhere on Earth. Keep it within the max size (~16 km²) for fastest response. Olive regions like Spain, Tunisia, Morocco, Italy, and Greece are ideal.

02 🛰️

GEE Fetches Images

Google Earth Engine computes cloud-free Sentinel-2 composites for Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter on the fly. Fresh imagery from the ESA Copernicus archive.

03 🧠

AI Analyzes 56 Bands

Our EfficientNet-B4 U-Net model analyzes all 4 seasonal images (56 bands total) to identify olive grove phenology — the unique seasonal signature that separates olives from almonds or palms.

04 🫒

Groves Appear Live

Green overlays mark every olive grove in your selection. Get instant coverage statistics — percentage of area, estimated hectares, and a breakdown of all 7 land classes.
